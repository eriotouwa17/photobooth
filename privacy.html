JC Smiles Photobooth – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

JC Smiles Photobooth is desktop photobooth software operated by JC Smiles at events. This policy explains what the software does with photos and with the Google account it is connected to.

Photos taken at the booth

  • Photos, animated GIFs and photo layouts are saved on the booth computer.
  • If the event uses online sharing, these files are uploaded to a Google Drive account belonging to JC Smiles (or to the event host, if they connected their own account).
  • Each session's files go into their own folder, shared as "anyone with the link can view". The booth shows that link as a QR code so guests can download their photos. Anyone who has the link can view that session's photos.
  • We do not sell photos or use them for advertising. To have your photos removed, contact us at the email below with the session number shown on the booth screen.

Google account data

  • The software requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. This lets it create folders and upload files that it made itself. It cannot see, read or change any other files in the Google Drive.
  • It reads the connected account's email address only to show which account is connected.
  • The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the booth computer and is never sent to anyone other than Google.
  • The software does not access Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the booth's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
